Clouded out? Chase the gap.

The eclipse runs 18:08–20:01. Deepest around 19:05. If your sky is grey, there may be a thinner patch within half an hour of you — this page checks the forecast in every direction and points you at it.

Decide by 17:00. Be parked and out of the car by 18:00. If you can't, stay where you are — 90% of the Sun disappearing behind thin cloud still beats sitting in traffic and missing the whole thing. Never look anywhere near the Sun while driving, and drivers: eyes on the road, the passengers do the looking.

    Forecast sampled at 12, 25 and 40 km in twelve directions from your spot, for the hour of deepest eclipse (when the most Sun is hidden).     Or watch it live — totality included

    Scotland gets 90%+ coverage. Spain gets totality — the Moon covering the whole Sun, revealing the corona: the Sun's wispy outer atmosphere, a halo you can never see any other time. These streams are free; times are UK (BST).
